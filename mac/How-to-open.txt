BIN DIFF
Compares two Avid bins. Clips are linked by their Tape name, and every other
column (Name, Duration, Start, End, Tracks, Scene, Take, Comments, ...) is
compared. Duration differences are also ranked, biggest first.

FIRST TIME ONLY
The app isn't from the App Store, so macOS blocks it the first time:
  1. Drag "Bin Diff" into your Applications folder.
  2. Double-click it. macOS will say it can't be opened. Click Done or OK.
  3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
     "Open Anyway" next to "Bin Diff". Confirm with your password.
     (On macOS 13 or 14 you can instead right-click the app > Open > Open.)
After that it opens normally with a double-click.

USING IT
  1. Double-click the app. One window opens with Bin A and Bin B side by side.
  2. Click Choose... on each side, or drag the .avb bin files in from Finder
     (drag both at once to fill A and B together; the arrows button swaps them).
  3. Click Compare. The report opens in its own window.
  4. Under "Differences by column", tick the columns you want to see
     (Duration, Start, End, Scene, ...). The report updates as you tick.
     Bin Diff remembers your choice for next time.
  5. Click Export PDF... to save what you're looking at as a PDF.
     (More > Print, Export HTML, Export CSV, Open in Browser.)
  The main window stays open, so you can compare another pair straight away.

SETTINGS (gear button, or Bin Diff > Settings...)
  Columns: which columns are compared (new columns are added as you compare bins).
  Report:  which sections appear, and the duration table's columns.
  General: where PDFs and saved reports go, optional automatic saving, and
           duration/tape matching options.
  Your settings are kept when you quit or update.

UPDATES
  The app checks for a new version once a day and offers to install it.
  You can also use Bin Diff > Check for Updates...
  For updates to install themselves, keep the app in your Applications folder.

PROBLEMS?
  Use the "Report a Bug..." button. It opens a ready-to-send GitHub issue
  (needs a free GitHub account).

Exports are saved in Documents > Bin Diff Reports unless you choose another folder.
